Privacy Policy

jUNK! Robot Warehouse · Last updated: November 3, 2026

Short version: jUNK does not collect, store or share any personal data.

What we collect

Nothing. The game has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no in-app purchases, and it does not connect to the internet.

What stays on your device

The game saves a few things on your device so it can work: your high scores and the name you type for them, your progress (unlocked robots in the Archive, Arena best wave) and your settings. Some builds may also keep simple gameplay statistics, such as how long each wave took, in a local file. This information never leaves your device, we cannot see it, and it is deleted when you delete the app.

Third parties

jUNK does not include advertising or analytics services and does not share data with third parties. If you contact us for support, we only use what you send us to answer you.

Children

Because the game collects no personal information, it collects none from children either.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact us.
