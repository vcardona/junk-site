The game

jUNK is a secret military robot that fought in both World Wars and was left to rust in a warehouse full of forgotten weapons projects. When a shipment of reprogrammed war robots gets a bad software update, they wake up in war mode, right inside a warehouse full of weapons.

Fight through 14 waves and 6 bosses in Boss Mode, unlock the endless Arena, and fill the Archive with every robot you destroy. Pay once: the whole game is yours. jUNK was born in 2014 and has been rebuilt from scratch for today's phones.

FAQ

How do I unlock the Arena?

Beat Boss Mode: survive all 14 waves and defeat Black Riot.

How do I play?

Move with the joystick on the left, hold the big button to fire the minigun, tap for missiles and grenades. The How To Play screen in the game shows everything, including what the arrows on the screen edge mean.

Does the game have ads or purchases?

No. jUNK is a one-time purchase with no ads, no in-app purchases and no account.

Where are my high scores saved?

On your device only. Deleting the app deletes your scores and progress.
